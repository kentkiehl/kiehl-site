The Kiehl Lab is part of the Mind Research Network, a nonprofit organization. Gifts are made to the Mind Research Network.

Give online

Online gifts are made on the Mind Research Network's own donation page.

Donate at mrn.org ↗

Give by mail

Checks can be mailed to: Mind Research Network, 1101 Yale Blvd. NE, Albuquerque, NM 87106. A donation form is available on the Mind Research Network donation page.

Questions

To discuss a gift in support of the lab's work, write to kkiehl@mrn.org.

What gifts support

Brain-imaging research on psychopathy, traumatic brain injury, substance use and related conditions, carried out with the lab's mobile MRI in prisons and treatment facilities, and the training of students and postdoctoral researchers.
