Principal investigator

Kent A. Kiehl, PhD

Kent A. Kiehl, PhD, is Executive Science Officer and Director of Mobile Imaging at the nonprofit Mind Research Network, an affiliate of Lovelace Biomedical and Environmental Research Institute, and Professor of Psychology, Neurosciences and Law at the University of New Mexico in Albuquerque. He uses clinical brain imaging to study psychopathy, psychotic disorders, traumatic brain injury, substance use and paraphilias.
