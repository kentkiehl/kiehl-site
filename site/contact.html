Lectures and media

Dr. Kiehl lectures to scientific, legal, correctional and general audiences on psychopathy, forensic brain imaging and neuroscience in the law. For speaking invitations and interviews, write to kkiehl@mrn.org. Past appearances are listed under Media & talks.

Joining the lab

Prospective graduate students apply through the Department of Psychology at the University of New Mexico. Prospective postdoctoral researchers and students interested in the laboratory's work are welcome to write with a CV and a short statement of research interests. Current members are listed on the People page.

Judicial education and consulting

Dr. Kiehl teaches neuroscience to judges and lawyers and consults on criminal and civil cases that involve neuroscience.
